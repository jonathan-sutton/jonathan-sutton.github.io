About Me

Jonathan Sutton is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at Aarhus University. His research focuses on augmented reality, visual perception, and computational eyewear, exploring how optical see-through head-mounted displays can augment human vision and guide visual attention. He holds a PhD from the University of Otago and publishes regularly in leading human-computer interaction venues such as ACM CHI and TOCHI.
